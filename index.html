The weft: our principles

  • Start with the job, not the feature.

    People pick up an app to make progress. Name that progress precisely and the rest follows.

  • Design for the moment, not the screen.

    People use apps on a packed bus, one-handed, half distracted. We design for that moment; the phone is only how it reaches them.

  • Calm is a feature.

    One main action per screen, one accent, nothing shouting. Calm interfaces earn trust.

The warps: our disciplines

  • Product strategy

    Framing the problem, the audience and the bet worth making.

  • Research

    Talking to people, reading the market, knowing which question to ask next.

  • Interaction design

    Flows, states, and the edge cases most teams only find in production.

  • Identity and systems

    Marks, motion, tokens and components that make a product feel whole.

  • Engineering

    React Native for iPhone and Android, built to the same standard as the design.
